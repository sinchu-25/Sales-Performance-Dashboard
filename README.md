# Sales Performance Dashboard
## Project Overview
The Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales, profit, quantity, discounts, and orders across different regions, customer segments, product categories, states, and sub-categories.
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of overall sales performance and helps identify the regions, categories, segments, and products contributing to business revenue and profit.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** 2.30M
- **Total Profit:** 286.40K
- **Total Quantity:** 38K
- **Total Discount:** 1.56K
- **Total Orders:** 10K

## Dashboard Analysis
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Segment
- Sales by State
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Profit by Category

## Key Insights
- The **West region** contributes the highest sales among the regions.
- **Technology** is the highest-performing category in terms of sales.
- The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of sales.
- **Phones and Chairs** are among the highest-selling sub-categories.
- **Technology** generates the highest profit among the product categories.
- The dashboard allows users to analyze sales performance using interactive filters for **Region, Segment, and Category**.

## Business Objective
The objective of this dashboard is to provide a clear overview of sales and profitability and help businesses identify high-performing regions, categories, customer segments, and product sub-categories for better decision-making.
